## What is VoilaTile?

- **Snap Mode** — hint-based snapping with minimal keystrokes  
- **Jump Mode** — jump focus across windows/monitors  
- **Look Mode** — preview/inspect without committing  
- **Settings** — layouts, hints & shortcuts, appearance

## Keyboard Cheatsheet

- **Open Snap overlay:** <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>
- **Type hints:** Press the characters shown on a window to snap it
- **Cancel overlay:** <kbd>Esc</kbd>
