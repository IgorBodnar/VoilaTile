## Recommended First-Time Setup

1. **Pick a base layout** under *Settings → Layouts* and assign it to each monitor.  
2. **Choose your hint pool** under *Settings → Hints & Shortcuts* (e.g., `ASDFGHJKL;`).  
3. **Bind a global toggle** for the Snap overlay (e.g., ++Alt+Space++).  
4. **Adjust contrast** under *Appearance* for your display/lighting.

> Tip: If a window’s hint clashes with another, VoilaTile resolves it automatically — but a balanced hint pool (no vowels or repeated shapes) keeps hints short and readable.
