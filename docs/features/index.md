## Quick Concepts

- **Zones & Layouts**  
  Define how your screen is partitioned. Multiple monitors supported.
- **Hints**  
  Short character sequences that uniquely identify a window/zone.
- **Overlays**  
  Lightweight, DPI-aware overlays that stay out of your way.
